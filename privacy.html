Privacy

This page explains how the three and a half productions website handles information.

Browsing this website

We do not ask you to create an account or submit personal information. This website does not include analytics, contact forms or a mailing list. Its own code does not save cookies or information in your browser.

When you visit this site on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security. You can read more in GitHub’s hosting information and its privacy statement.

Videos and external links

The trailer is served as a video file from this website and plays only when you choose to start it. Our YouTube links take you to YouTube; there is no embedded YouTube player. Once you visit another website, its own privacy practices apply.

Advertising

The banner currently promotes our own game. Google advertising is not enabled, and this website does not request Google advertising scripts in this configuration.

This website uses Google AdSense. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies, device information and similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements. Advertising cookies can enable ads based on previous visits to this and other websites.

Where applicable, an advertising privacy message lets you manage consent. You can also manage personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center, and learn about participating vendors’ opt-out choices at YourAdChoices.

The advertising privacy message contains the participating vendor list and links to vendors’ privacy information. Use Privacy and cookie settings to reopen that message when available.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Declining personalised advertising may still allow non-personalised or limited ads, depending on the choices offered and applicable requirements.

The game

This information covers the studio website. Little Railway Works is an entirely offline Android game; the website’s advertising is separate from the game.
